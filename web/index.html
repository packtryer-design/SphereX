    How to read this

    SPHEREx photographs the sky in many infrared colors that our eyes can't see. Each layer is the same patch of sky in one color.

    Press ▶ or Blink to flip between colors. Things that switch on and off are made of something that shines in that color.

    Click any spot to see how bright it is in every layer.
